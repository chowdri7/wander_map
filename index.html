Plan a trip in three steps

Add every place you might pass through, join them with the ways to travel between them, then build plans and compare them.

  1. Add your placesHome, the airport, a station, the city you are going to. Each place becomes one row.
  2. Join them with legsFor each way to get between two places: how you travel, when it leaves and arrives, and the fare.
  3. Make a planName it, pick where you start and finish, and choose the way wherever the routes split. Make as many plans as you like and compare them.

Your plans are saved only in this browser. Use Export to keep a copy or move them to another device.

View your trip on the go

On a phone, Wander Map shows your timeline. Plans are made on a computer: choose Export there to save a file, send the file to this phone, then tap Import plans and pick it.
